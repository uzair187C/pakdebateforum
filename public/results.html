WUDC Sofia — Scholarship Support

Impact

PDF committed partial sponsorship of 20–40% of cost toward the LUMS team's trip to the World Universities Debating Championship in Sofia. PDF raised $1,200 through the Pak Debate Forum Open to support the journey. Ahmad Bin Tahir and Daud Ali Kharal of LUMS received the scholarship.

WUDC · Scholarship · Sofia, Bulgaria
