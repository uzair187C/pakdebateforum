About PDF

Built for Access. Proven on the World Stage.

Pak Debate Forum is an international debate organization and academy — founded in Pakistan to increase representation, build training infrastructure, and fund access to the global debating circuit.

Why PDF Exists

Access to the International Circuit

PDF was founded to increase Pakistani representation at major international tournaments and to build the training, funding, and organizational infrastructure that makes sustained participation possible.

From running Pakistan's Pre-WSDC with 37 nations to sponsoring debaters at WUDC Sofia — PDF creates pathways where none existed before.

“Debate is not about winning arguments. It is about sharpening minds, fostering truth, and giving voice to conviction.”

Pak Debate Forum Founding Principle

How PDF Creates Access

Three Mechanisms

Competition

PDF organises tournaments that serve as qualifying and preparatory events for international circuits — from the Pre-WSDC (37 nations, 60 teams) to the Ramadan Debates Open and Blackstone Invitational.

Training

The PDF Academy runs structured programmes from beginner to advanced level. Community Workshops at UoL, UCP, UMT, and CMH Lahore extend training reach into institutional debating societies.

Funding & Opportunity

$2,200 in fee waivers at the Pre-WSDC. $1,200 raised through the PDF Open for WUDC Sofia. $700 FLTRP community scholarship. PDF builds financial access where barriers exist.

Impact

Where PDF Has Made a Difference

Krabi ABP — Funding Participation

PDF's Pakistan Pre-ABP 2024 was explicitly positioned as a means of funding Pakistani participation at the Asian British Parliamentary Championship in Krabi, Thailand.

Krabi ABP · 2024

WUDC Sofia — Scholarship Support

PDF committed partial sponsorship of 20–40% of cost toward the LUMS team's trip to WUDC Sofia. Ahmad Bin Tahir and Daud Ali Kharal received the scholarship. PDF raised $1,200 through the Pak Debate Forum Open.

Raised through PDF Open
