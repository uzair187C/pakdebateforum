About PDF

Pakistan's Home for Debate Excellence

Pak Debate Forum exists to democratize access to world-class debate education and build the next generation of articulate, confident, and critical thinkers across Pakistan.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

Pak Debate Forum was founded on a simple belief: every young Pakistani deserves access to world-class training in the skills that shape leaders — argument, communication, research, and the courage to speak.

We provide structured, progressive programmes from beginner to advanced level, delivered by coaches with national and international competitive experience.

“Debate is not about winning arguments. It is about sharpening minds, fostering truth, and giving voice to conviction.”

Pak Debate Forum Founding Principle

What Sets Us Apart

Built Different

Competition-First Approach

Our curriculum is designed around the formats that matter — British Parliamentary, Asian Parliamentary, and World Schools. Everything we teach has real tournament application.

Institutional Quality

We don't run one-off workshops. Our multi-week cohort programmes provide the depth and continuity needed to build lasting skills and competitive readiness.

National Reach

From Karachi to Islamabad, Lahore to Peshawar — we serve debaters across Pakistan through both in-person and online programmes.

Expert Coaches

Every PDF coach brings verified competitive experience — national champions, international circuit veterans, and trained adjudicators who have been where you want to go.

Open Resources

Motion databases, format guides, video libraries, and training materials — available to the entire Pakistani debate community, not just enrolled students.

Results That Matter

Our students consistently place at national and international tournaments. We measure success by your growth, not our branding.
