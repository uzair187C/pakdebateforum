# PAK DEBATE FORUM (PDF) — 31 ABOUT PAGE IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION

Status: Dedicated About / Institutional Identity experience implementation contract.

Purpose:
Define the About experience as the place where visitors understand what Pak Debate Forum (PDF) is, why it exists, what it believes in, who is behind it, and how the institution has developed.

The About experience must feel:
- credible
- human
- purposeful
- intellectually serious
- warm without becoming casual
- premium
- unmistakably PDF

It must NOT feel like:
- a generic corporate About Us page
- a founder biography dumped onto a page
- a wall of text
- a charity template
- a startup landing page
- an oversized timeline with no narrative

This specification governs:
- institutional storytelling
- mission/vision
- values
- history
- leadership/team presentation
- impact
- partnerships
- photography
- timeline
- calls to action
- motion
- responsive behavior
- accessibility
- SEO
- performance
- content integrity
- component architecture
- QA

Use with the global PDF design system and all previously approved page specifications.

---

# 1. ABOUT ROLE IN THE PDF ECOSYSTEM

The ecosystem should now read:

```text
Homepage  = identity / first impression
About     = meaning / trust
Events    = participation
Academy   = development
Resources = knowledge
```

The About page answers:

```text
Who is PDF?
Why does PDF exist?
What does PDF stand for?
Who makes it happen?
What has it achieved?
Where is it going?
```

---

# 2. PRIMARY EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLE

The About page is not an information dump.

It is a guided institutional story.

Recommended narrative:

```text
WHAT WE ARE
↓
WHY WE EXIST
↓
WHAT WE BELIEVE
↓
HOW WE DEVELOPED
↓
WHO WE ARE
↓
WHAT WE HAVE DONE
↓
WHERE WE ARE GOING
↓
HOW YOU CAN CONNECT
```

Only use sections supported by actual information.

---

# 3. TONE

The writing and visual language should communicate:

```text
confidence
purpose
intelligence
discipline
community
ambition
```

Avoid:
- exaggerated corporate language
- empty superlatives
- invented claims
- overuse of "revolutionary", "world-class", "transformative"
- artificial emotional language

---

# 4. VISUAL RELATIONSHIP TO HOMEPAGE

Retain:
- near-black foundation
- warm white typography
- restrained gold
- editorial spacing
- premium typography
- subtle cinematic depth

Reduce:
- dramatic 3D centerpiece
- intense spotlighting
- large decorative effects

About is more human and editorial.

---

# 5. ABOUT HERO

Hero hierarchy:

```text
eyebrow
H1
short institutional statement
optional supporting visual
```

The hero should communicate institutional confidence immediately.

---

# 6. HERO VISUAL

- authentic PDF event photography
- leadership/team image
- debate-stage photography
- carefully composed institutional image
- restrained abstract visual

real PDF imagery > generic stock imagery.

---

# 7. HERO IMAGE TREATMENT

Possible:

- dark overlay
- subtle warm grade
- edge vignette
- controlled crop

Avoid excessive cinematic grading that makes people look unnatural.

---

# 8. HERO MOBILE

Mobile should place:

```text
H1
statement
visual
```

in a deliberate vertical sequence.

Do not crop important people out.

---



# 11. MISSION DESIGN

- large statement

# 12. VISION

---

# 14. VALUES

```text

value
```

# 17. VALUE MOTION

---

# 18. HISTORY

```text

I know where I can go next.
```

It should feel like an institution with a real story—not a template with an About tab.
