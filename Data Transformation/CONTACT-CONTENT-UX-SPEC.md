# PDF CONTACT — CONTENT & UX SPECIFICATION

## 1. PURPOSE

Contact is the human bridge between interest and participation.

## 2. AUDIENCE PATHS

Possible:
```text
General inquiry
Academy
Events
Partnership
Institutional collaboration
Media
Other
```

Only use supported categories.

## 3. INTERNATIONAL AUDIENCE

Contact should welcome visitors outside Pakistan.

Do not assume:
- Pakistani nationality
- Pakistani institution
- Pakistani phone number
unless required by the inquiry.

## 4. HERO

Simple:
- clear heading
- welcoming statement
- direct channels

No podium centerpiece.

## 5. FORM

Minimum:
```text
Name
Email
Inquiry type
Message
```

Organization may be added when useful for partnership/institutional inquiries.

## 6. PARTNERSHIPS

Where supported, allow:
```text
institution
country/region
interest
proposed collaboration
```

## 7. RESPONSE EXPECTATION

Only promise timing that PDF can actually meet.

## 8. FINAL FALLBACK

Provide direct official email/contact channels where available.
