# PDF HOME — CONTENT & UX RESTRUCTURE SPECIFICATION

## 1. ROLE

Home introduces PDF and proves enough credibility to make deeper exploration compelling.

The podium is NOT the Home centerpiece.

## 2. HERO

Hero hierarchy:

```text
small label
↓
large headline
↓
short positioning statement
↓
primary CTA
secondary CTA if useful
```

Use a rich photographic/atmospheric background rather than an empty black field.

- Explore Academy

## 12. IMAGE REQUIREMENTS

Priority:
1. hero atmosphere/photo
2. WUDC/LUMS story visual
3. Academy visual
4. current-event visual
5. community/network visual

Do not use random stock imagery.

## 13. MOTION

Hero: medium cinematic.
Proof: restrained.
Case study: subtle image reveal.
Metrics: gentle reveal, no exaggerated count-up.
Footer: quiet.

