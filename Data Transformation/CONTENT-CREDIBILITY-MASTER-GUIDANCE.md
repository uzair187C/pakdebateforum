# PAK DEBATE FORUM (PDF) — CONTENT & CREDIBILITY MASTER GUIDANCE

Status: Content strategy and page-direction layer.
Purpose: Turn PDF's verified track record into a coherent, credible, international-facing website experience.

## 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The website must stop communicating only:

> “We are a debate organization that runs events and an Academy.”

It should communicate:

> “Pak Debate Forum is an active debating institution building access, training, competition, international participation, adjudication standards, and community connections.”

The site should make a visitor understand PDF's scale, seriousness, access mission, international relevance, and pathways to participation without becoming a wall of statistics.

## 2. AUDIENCE PRIORITY

The website is NOT Pakistan-only.

Primary audience categories:
1. International/foreign students and debaters who want to train, compete, participate, or connect.
2. Pakistani students/debaters.
3. International coaches, judges, institutions, and tournament communities.
4. Universities and student organizations looking for training, events, partnerships, or adjudication development.
5. Parents/educators/institutional decision-makers where relevant.

Pakistan is part of PDF's identity and founding mission. International access is part of its ambition and operating environment.

Positioning:

```text
Rooted in Pakistan
Connected to the international circuit
Open to a wider debating community
```

## 3. CORE STORY

The content system should repeatedly reinforce:

### Access
PDF exists to make serious/international debating opportunities more accessible.

### Action
PDF creates tournaments, training, workshops, scholarships, fee waivers, and other real opportunities.

### International reach
PDF participates in and connects with international debating environments.

### Standards
PDF works around serious competitive and adjudication standards.

### Development
PDF develops speakers, judges, institutions, and a broader debating community.

## 4. PROOF HIERARCHY

### Tier 1 — Transformational proof
Use prominently:
- founding mission around international access and representation
- WUDC Sofia scholarship/support story
- $1,200 fundraiser for the LUMS team's trip
- FLTRP community scholarship
- prior Academy cohort
- 2026 Community Workshops
- international representation/judging
- caliber of invited/associated judges

### Tier 2 — Scale proof
Use selectively:
- 37 nations at 2026 Pre-WSDC
- 60 teams
- 28 official National Debate Teams
- $2,200 in fee waivers
- recurring tournament formats/series
- institutional collaborations

### Tier 3 — Detailed proof
Place deeper:
- individual judge credentials
- individual workshop hosts
- tournament histories
- specific chronology

## 5. DIFFERENTIATOR

The strongest differentiator is NOT:

> “We organize debate tournaments.”

It is:

> PDF uses competition, training, partnerships, and community infrastructure to create access to higher-level debating opportunities.

The website should demonstrate this through real examples.

## 6. CREDIBILITY FORMAT RULE

```text
Mission           → large editorial statement
Scale             → restrained typography-led metrics
Impact            → case study
Internationality  → map/story/journey
People            → curated profile/credential cards
Partnerships      → ecosystem/network section
History           → timeline
Events            → dynamic catalogue
Training          → Academy pathway
Results           → evidence/archive
```

Do not turn everything into cards.

## 7. CLAIM SAFETY

The supplied source was cross-checked against PDF's own posts but remains self-reported rather than third-party audited.

Safe factual claims include:
- 37 nations participated in the 2026 Pre-WSDC.
- 60 teams participated.
- $2,200 in fee waivers were awarded.
- PDF raised $1,200 through the Pak Debate Forum Open to support the LUMS WUDC trip.
- FLTRP granted $700 toward the community scholarship initiative.
- PDF sent subsidised judges to WSDC 2026 and recorded the listed judging outcomes.

Verify internally before publishing as superlatives:
- largest
- first-ever
- first time ever
- only
- biggest
- most
- unprecedented

## 8. LANGUAGE

Use:
- confident
- specific
- evidence-led
- international
- human
- intellectually serious

Avoid:
- generic NGO language
- unsupported “world-class”
- Pakistan-only framing
- vague claims without evidence

## 9. INTERNATIONAL LANGUAGE

Prefer:
- international circuit
- global debating community
- international opportunities
- universities and institutions
- students and debaters
- speakers and adjudicators

Use “Pakistani” when a fact specifically concerns Pakistan.

Do not repeatedly frame genuine international opportunities as “for Pakistani students.”

## 10. CONTENT LOOP

```text
PDF organizes
↓
PDF creates opportunities
↓
People use those opportunities
↓
Some reach international stages
↓
Those experiences feed back into training/community
↓
PDF grows the ecosystem
```

## 11. PAGE ROLES

Home: evidence + curiosity + pathways.
About: institutional story + credibility.
Academy: education + development + international relevance.
Events: active ecosystem + practical discovery.
Programs: fit + outcomes + action.
Results: proof + competitive/adjudication record.
Resources: authority + knowledge.
Contact: conversion + human communication.

## 12. NETWORK

Do not label every relationship “sponsor.”

Distinguish actual:
- partner
- host
- co-organizer
- funder
- community organization
- institutional collaborator

## 13. PHOTO PRINCIPLE

Priority:
1. authentic PDF photography
2. approved institutional/event photography
3. carefully generated brand/atmospheric visuals only when needed

Avoid generic stock photography as the default.

## 14. FINAL CONTENT TEST

A strong visitor reaction should be:

> “I understand what PDF is, I can see evidence that it is serious, I understand how I can participate, and I want to explore further.”
