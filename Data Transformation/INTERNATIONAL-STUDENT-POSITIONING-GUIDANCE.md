# PDF — INTERNATIONAL AUDIENCE & STUDENT POSITIONING GUIDANCE

## 1. CORE RULE

PDF is rooted in Pakistan but must NOT be presented as a Pakistan-only organization.

A foreign student should be able to land on the site and think:

> “This is relevant to me.”

## 2. LANGUAGE

Prefer:
```text
students
debaters
speakers
adjudicators
institutions
universities
international circuit
global debating community
```

Use “Pakistani” only where the specific fact concerns Pakistan.

## 3. WHAT FOREIGN STUDENTS SHOULD UNDERSTAND

Within a few pages, a foreign student should understand:
- what PDF is
- what it does
- what debate environments it works in
- what training exists
- what events/opportunities exist
- whether participation is open to them
- how international PDF is
- how to contact/join

## 4. INTERNATIONAL PROOF

Use specific evidence:
- 37 nations / 60 teams at 2026 Pre-WSDC
- international judges
- WSDC Nairobi
- Oxford WSDC judging
- WUDC Sofia scholarship support
- international tournament contexts fileciteturn6file0

Do not merely say “global.”

## 5. PAKISTAN'S ROLE

Use:

```text
Rooted in Pakistan
+
Connected to the international circuit
+
Open to a broader debating community
```

Do not erase the founding mission.

## 6. ACADEMY

Academy should feel internationally relevant.

Training can be framed around:
```text
better debating
stronger adjudication
competitive readiness
international exposure
institutional development
```
without guaranteeing outcomes.

## 7. EVENTS / PROGRAMS

State eligibility, location, format, and application details clearly.

## 8. CONTACT

Use country-neutral copy unless geography is an actual requirement.

## 9. FINAL TEST

A foreign student should be able to answer:
```text
Do I understand PDF?
Could I participate?
Does PDF operate internationally?
Do I know what to do next?
```
